The year is 2026, but war meant the last human died in 1999. Robots are now the sole inhabitants of the earth, including Robo, whose sole purpose is to create instant coffee. However, he needs repairs fast and must find parts from the era he was built in if he is to continue on his mission.

Play as Robo and collect pieces of old technology and kill enemy robots to repair yourself!

Objective: 
* Collect 25 parts within the allotted time, otherwise enemies become faster and stronger! 
* Your health decays overtime and parts you collect heal you. 
* Kill enemies and break PCs for parts. Win by finding all the parts Robo needs!

Gameplay:
* W - Up
* A - Left
* S - Down
* D - Right
* Space - Jump
* Enter - Attack
